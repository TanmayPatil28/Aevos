Office Hours: Tuesdays and Thursdays, 2PM - 4PM

Course Objectives:
This course covers fundamental data structures including arrays, linked lists, stacks, queues, hash tables, trees, and graphs.
Students will learn algorithm analysis (Big-O notation) and essential algorithms like sorting (QuickSort, MergeSort) and graph traversal (BFS, DFS).
